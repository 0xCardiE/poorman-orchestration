Goal:
- Build a digest view that turns stored research into a useful summary.

Acceptance criteria:
- Add a digest dashboard grouped by topic.
- Surface recent additions, unresolved questions, and conflicting claims.
- Keep digest generation explainable and rule-based.
- Make the digest useful even without AI integration.
- Confirm the app still builds successfully.

Constraints:
- Avoid opaque scoring systems.
- Keep the first digest logic deterministic and easy to inspect.
- Focus on usefulness over sophistication.
