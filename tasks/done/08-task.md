## Goal
Polish the MVP so it is stable, documented, and ready for short play sessions.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Obvious gameplay bugs from earlier tasks are fixed without large refactors.
- HUD, controls text, and restart flow are clear enough for a first-time player.
- Basic mobile controls are added only if they are easy and do not destabilize desktop play; otherwise this is explicitly deferred in `README.md`.
- `README.md` reflects setup, controls, current feature status, and any known limitations.
- Final build passes, and tests/lint are run where relevant.

## Constraints
- Focus on practical polish, not new major features.
- Keep the codebase modular and avoid broad rewrites late in the sequence.
- Do not add online features, accounts, stores, or complex physics.
- Prefer small targeted fixes that keep the game playable at all times.
