## Goal
Implement match rules for scoring, a visible timer, and a restart match flow.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Goals are detected and the score updates correctly.
- A visible match timer counts down or up consistently during play.
- The match can end or reset cleanly and the player has a clear restart flow.
- HUD information for score and timer is visible during the match.
- User-visible match flow and controls are documented in `README.md`.

## Constraints
- Keep match rules lightweight and suitable for short play sessions.
- Avoid introducing tournament structure, commentary, or backend features.
- Reuse existing scenes where possible instead of adding unnecessary new flow.
- Verify the build before completing the task.
