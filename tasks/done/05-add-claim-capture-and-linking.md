Goal:
- Add claim capture and simple claim relationships.

Acceptance criteria:
- Allow claims to be created and linked to one or more sources.
- Support relationship labels such as support, contradict, and neutral.
- Show linked claims in source and topic views.
- Keep claim editing understandable and easy to maintain.
- Confirm the app still builds successfully.

Constraints:
- Keep the relationship model simple and explicit.
- Do not introduce complex graph tooling.
- Prefer transparent rule-based behavior over automation.
